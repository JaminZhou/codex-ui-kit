## Capture method

The capture used one temporary isolated loopback CDP process launched with
`open -n` and a separate temporary Chromium profile. It read only the fixed
New chat, Projects, and Pull requests routes; no prompt was sent and no
resource or plugin was selected. The native project context-menu item list is
explicitly unavailable at the renderer/CDP boundary. The temporary process was
closed after capture and its profile was moved to the macOS Trash.
