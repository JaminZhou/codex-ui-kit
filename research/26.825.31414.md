## Plan writing and completion follow-up

A third disposable task requested only a read-only Plan and did not use files,
Terminal, or external tools. Separate 3-, 5-, 8-, and 10-step runs established
that the active plan is owned by the Composer dock rather than the message

- The active trigger reads `Step n / total`, uses a 12×12 progress ring, and
  measures 106.671875×38px for `Step 1 / 8`. Its 32px dock keeps the 736×98
  Composer at y=706 in the 1180×820 reference.
- Hover, focus, or click opens a `role=tooltip` list immediately above the
  trigger. The eight-step sample is 95.578125×200px with 8px padding, 15px
  radius, 16px rows, 8px row gaps, 16×16 status rings, and 14/16px weight-400
  text. The ten-step sample is 248px tall, confirming the same row formula
  rather than a fixed eight-row card.
- Progress advances from `Step 1 / 8` to `Step 5 / 8`. Once every step is
  complete, the trigger and tooltip disappear; the turn then settles from
  `Working for …` to `Worked for …` before the final answer.
- The public replay consumes the schema-valid `turn/plan/updated` notification,
  maps `pending`, `inProgress`, and `completed`, and rejects updates owned by a
  different turn. Browser/CDP and Electron gate active, expanded, progressing,
  all-complete, and settled states. Three reviewed 1180×820 baselines cover the
  active, expanded, and settled frames. A tight foreground-only comparison
  against the local-only eight-step product capture passes at 2.8869% under a
  4.5% ceiling; geometry remains independently exact-gated.
