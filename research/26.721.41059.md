CDP covers Renderer structure and behavior only. Native window composition,
system dialogs, app menus, title-bar behavior, real native resizing, and other
main-process surfaces still require manual or separate Electron-level
acceptance.

## Clean-room boundary

No bundled JavaScript, CSS, source maps, fonts, images, icons, sounds, private
IPC, authentication material, or service endpoint is included. Package assets
are not treated as reusable source. Public markup, styles, APIs, and assets
must continue to be independently implemented.
