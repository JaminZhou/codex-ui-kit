## Project Section submenu drift

The project action boundary still begins with a frozen
`electronBridge.showContextMenu` provider, but the provider and the final
native menu are distinct layers on this build:

- the provider contains `Unpin`, `Edit`, `Section`, `Reveal in Finder`,
  `Create permanent worktree`, `Archive chats`, and `Remove project`, plus
  three separators;
- `Section` owns a submenu and has no direct `onSelect`; the sampled profile
  has no custom sections, so its submenu contains only `New section…`;
- the rendered AppKit menu filters the provider-only `Reveal in Finder` row in
  this context. Its visible six actions are `Unpin`, `Edit`, `Section`,
  `Create permanent worktree`, `Archive chats`, and `Remove project`;
- the layer-101 native main menu is exactly 252×187 and the one-row submenu is
  exactly 118×34;
- the `Section` SVG is captured from the provider as one exact 16×16 source,
  recorded with SHA-256
  `a3eb21ed39b3b2483c2803b81743cd34ff4d1b53ccd134d664177490ab983a17`.

visible composition. `MenuSubmenu` now exposes an accessible submenu label and
