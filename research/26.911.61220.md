## Capture method

The capture used one temporary isolated loopback CDP process on port `9973`
and a unique profile under `/private/tmp/codex-ui-kit-*`. Existing Codex
windows were not navigated or resized. The process was selected by URL,
viewport area, shell landmarks, and visible-control density, then terminated
and its temporary profile removed after the evidence was copied. Navigation
was limited to the allowlisted New chat, Projects, Pull requests, sidebar,
Help, and fixed viewport interactions.

The script hashed `app.asar` before and after Renderer sampling and proved the
same device, inode, size, timestamp, and SHA-256. No prompt, project,
repository, account, or remote resource was submitted or persisted.
