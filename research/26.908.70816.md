## Capture method

The capture used one temporary isolated loopback CDP process on port `9957`
and a unique profile under `/private/tmp/codex-ui-kit-*`. Existing Codex
windows were not navigated or resized. The process was selected by URL,
viewport area, shell landmarks, and visible-control density, then terminated
and its temporary profile moved to the macOS Trash after capture.

The script hashed `app.asar` before and after Renderer sampling and proved the
same device, inode, size, timestamp, and SHA-256. It performed only the
allowlisted New chat, Projects, Pull requests, sidebar, Help, and fixed
viewport interactions; no prompt, project, repository, account, or remote
resource was submitted or persisted.
